### Core Intelligence Architecture

1. **Top 5 Leaderboard (All Categories)**: Side-by-side executive view of confirmed and rising designs across every category.
2. **The 4-Family Concordance Engine**:
   - **S (Search)**: Google monthly volume & YoY growth vs category baseline.
   - **M (Marketplaces)**: Amazon *"bought in past month"* volume + Google Shopping multi-merchant reviews (SerpApi compliant, no illegal scraping).
   - **T (Sell-Through)**: Shopify catalogue sell-outs vs each individual store's baseline rate.
   - **O (Social)**: Instagram likes vs creator baseline + YouTube video velocity.
3. **Audience & Life Stages**: Demographic attribution inferred from search modifiers, sizing curves, and styling intent.
4. **Keyword Magic**: High-intent search terms grouped by volume, growth, and query intent.
